TrustHire AI is an AI-powered candidate authenticity and skill verification platform designed to overcome the limitations of traditional ATS systems. Instead of relying mainly on keyword-based resume screening, TrustHire AI analyzes resumes to extract candidate skills, education, experience, and projects and uses semantic AI to match candidates with job requirements. It aims to help recruiters identify genuinely relevant candidates while reducing the problems of fake resumes, false claims, AI-generated content, plagiarized projects, and time-consuming manual verification.

The platform evaluates candidates through multiple dimensions, including ATS/Semantic Matching, Skill Evaluation, Project Evaluation, Experience, Education, Technical Skill Verification, AI-Generated Content Detection, Plagiarism Analysis, Trust Score, and Fraud Indicators. Candidates can be evaluated through AI-powered technical questions and assessments to provide evidence for their claimed skills. TrustHire AI generates a Trust Score to help HR evaluate candidate credibility and hiring risk, while the overall evaluation report provides explanations, strengths, weaknesses, and fraud-related indicators.

TrustHire AI provides separate workflows for Candidates, HR, and Admins, including registration, job applications, resume upload, technical question answering, job posting, application management, AI reports, HR management, and system reports. The project uses React.js and Bootstrap for the frontend, Node.js and Express.js for the backend, Python FastAPI for AI services, and MySQL for database management, with AI/ML technologies such as BERT, SBERT, RoBERTa, Random Forest, and SHAP used as part of the proposed AI/ML architecture. Overall, TrustHire AI combines resume matching, candidate verification, technical skill assessment, authenticity analysis, and fraud-risk analysis into a unified recruitment platform.
